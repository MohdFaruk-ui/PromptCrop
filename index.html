What is PromptCrop OCR?
PromptCrop OCR is a secure, 100% offline Chrome extension that lets you drag a selection box over any video frame or webpage to instantly extract and copy text.
Does PromptCrop OCR send my screenshots to a server?
No. All image processing and OCR are executed entirely on your machine using local WebAssembly binaries, ensuring 100% data privacy and offline capability.
How does the offline OCR script auto-detection work?
It uses an optimized local Tesseract OCR engine that detects 9 languages simultaneously, including English, Bengali, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese, Hindi, Russian, and Arabic.
