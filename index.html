SnapTextify is a secure, 100% offline OCR Chrome extension powered by local WebAssembly binaries to instantly copy text and extract code from YouTube videos, images, and copy-restricted websites. Is my data really private?
Yes. OCR runs locally via WebAssembly inside a sealed browser sandbox. Your screenshots are processed locally and discarded. They are never uploaded to our servers.
Does it work on videos?
Yes — pause any video (YouTube, Udemy, Coursera, Vimeo, or any HTML5 player) and select the text to copy it instantly.
Do I need to sign up?
No. Free English OCR works immediately after installation without any registration or credit cards. An account or license key is only needed to unlock Premium features.
Which languages are supported?
SnapTextify supports English, Bengali, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Russian, and Arabic OCR auto-detection.
